# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in this repository.

This project is primarily a personal and experimental system. Contributions are welcome when they are **intentional, relevant, and human-driven**.

## General Participation Guidelines

- Issues, comments, and pull requests should be created by a **human**, with clear intent to discuss, improve, or question the system.
- **Automated, scripted, or LLM-generated content** (including unsolicited summaries, paraphrasing, or “helpful suggestions” posted without human review) is not welcome.
- Engagement intended primarily to boost visibility, reputation, or activity metrics will be removed.

Moderation decisions are made to keep discussion signal-rich and relevant to the project.

---

## Issues and Discussions

When opening an issue or starting a discussion:

- Describe **your setup or scenario** (OS, environment, relevant configuration).
- Explain **what you were trying to do**.
- Describe **what happened** and **what you expected**.
- Include logs, error messages, or timestamps where relevant.

Generic, auto-generated, or context-free issues may be closed or marked as spam without further discussion.

---

## Code Contributions

Code contributions are welcome when they meet the following criteria:

- They address a **specific, understood problem** or improvement.
- The contributor understands **why** the change is needed.
- The change is scoped and explained in human terms.

Please note:

- Pull requests generated entirely by automated tools or LLMs, without human understanding or review, are not accepted.
- Code submitted should be maintainable by someone other than the original author.
- Large or architectural changes should be discussed in an issue before implementation.

A pull request may be declined if it appears to be:
- drive-by automation,
- speculative refactoring,
- or disconnected from the project’s actual usage.

---

## Scope

This repository is not seeking:
- Automated reviews or summaries
- General advice unrelated to the system as implemented
- Contributions made without understanding the runtime context

Thanks for understanding.
